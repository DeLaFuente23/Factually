# Factually...

Three claims. Three verdicts. How well can you tell fact from fiction?

The world is full of facts so ridiculous they sound made up, half-truths that hide a catch, and convincing lies that seem perfectly reasonable. Factually... challenges you to tell them apart.

## Each round presents three surprising claims. 

Your job is to assign each one a verdict:

FACT! — It's true, exactly as stated.
KINDA? — There's some truth to it, but there's a catch.
BULLSH1T — Sounds convincing. Completely wrong.

There's exactly one of each verdict per round. Sounds easy? Don't get too confident.

You have three rounds to prove your instincts, uncover bizarre facts, and earn up to 10 points. Correct classifications earn points, partially correct deductions can still get you some credit, and three powerful wildcards can help you out when you're stuck—but using one costs you a third of a point.

Every claim comes with an explanation and sources, so even when you get it wrong, you might discover something worth sharing.

## Think you know what's true? Let's see.

Daily challenges are deterministically selected from the bundled JSON library using the UTC date. Card order is randomized and saved per browser. The client-side data can be inspected by visitors. Game progress and personal results are stored locally in the browser (versioned key `factually.v2`); they do not sync across devices. The initial library has 18 records drawn from the supplied Factually_Final.txt.
